# Stamped Energy: Marketing product context

register: brand

## Product purpose

Stamped Energy markets **Stamped Intelligence**: a software-first, read-only overlay on plant OT/IT for Indian energy-intensive manufacturers. It turns meters, SCADA/PLC/EMS, bills, and optional order context into **assigned prescriptions with ₹ impact**, then **verifies with evidence** and **improves based on decisions taken**.

## Users

Primary: plant directors, VP Operations, electrical HODs, CFOs at plants with ₹20 lakh+ monthly electricity bills (cement, steel, pharma, chemical, automotive).

State of mind: cost pressure, distrust of dashboards, need for clear next actions and proof.

## Value propositions

1. **Industry Energy Management:** load management and energy-efficiency prescriptions (MD, TOD, idle, HVAC, tariff).
2. **Asset Health Intelligence:** same stack flags equipment issues early.

One product, two pillars. Shared context (orders/departments) enables management Rx without becoming MES.

## Operating loop

Connect → Observe → Decide → Execute → Verify → **Improve**

Improve = continuous calibration from decisions taken and verified outcomes. Never “AI is learning about you.”

## Proof language

Lead with **Verified with evidence** (ops-cleared / calculated ledger). DISCOM bill confirmation is optional, not the hero claim. No invented fleet metrics; indicative ranges with disclaimer only.

## Site IA

Nav: Solutions · Platform · Industries · Case Studies · About · Contact

- `/solutions` hub + `/solutions/load-energy` + `/solutions/equipment-intelligence`
- `/platform` (ex How It Works)
- Case Studies, About, Contact preserved but distilled

## Anti-references

- Purple SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates, identical icon-card grids
- MES / CMMS / plant OS replacement claims
- Bill-verified badges from ops-only data
- Cloning Infinite Uptime or Greenovative brand visuals (structure inspiration only)

## Brand voice

Direct, industrial, rupee-first. Short sentences. No em dashes. Plant-floor credibility over software hype.
