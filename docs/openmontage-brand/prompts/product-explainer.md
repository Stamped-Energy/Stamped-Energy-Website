# Prompt: Product explainer (45–90s)

## Preconditions

Agent read-order in `../README.md`. Playbook: `stamped-industrial`.

## Brief

Produce a **60-second** animated explainer (default) for Stamped Intelligence.

**Audience:** Plant director / VP Ops / electrical head at a North India Band A plant (≥ ₹30L/month electricity).  
**Vertical default:** Automotive components (change if brief specifies cement/steel/pharma/chemical).  
**Outcome:** Viewer understands Stamped is a read-only decision layer that turns plant data into assigned ₹-scored prescriptions, verified with evidence.

## Structure

1. Hook (pain): fragmented data, insight without closure  
2. What Stamped is (one product, two pillars)  
3. Loop: Connect → Observe → Decide → Execute → Verify → Improve  
4. Hero UI: prescription card assemble (what / who / ₹ / evidence)  
5. CTA: Book a Discovery Call  

## Must

- ₹ not $  
- Verified with evidence; DISCOM optional  
- Ease-out only; coral on `#f7faf5`  
- Exact pillar names  

## Must not

- Invented fleet metrics  
- EMS/MES replacement  
- CVector marks or their % tiles  
- Em dashes
