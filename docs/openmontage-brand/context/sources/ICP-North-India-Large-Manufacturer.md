## 1. Universal hard gates (all tiers)

Every Stamped prospect must pass **all four** before active pipeline:

| # | Gate | Pass | Fail |
|---|------|------|------|
| G1 | **Captive manufacturing plant** with HT/EHT connection (not HQ, not pure trading/job-work) | Own factory, process equipment | Distributor, IT services, hospital, logistics fleet |
| G2 | **Electricity decision at plant/BU** | Plant Head or Electrical Head can run 60–90 day pilot | “Group IT / global procurement only” |
| G3 | **Data plane exists or obtainable** | EMS, SCADA, feeder meters, or DISCOM bills + production logs | No meters, refuses any data share |
| G4 | **ROI path to Stamped ACV** | Estimated bill supports ≥₹15L/year software value at 10–20% savings | Bill so small that ₹3–8L ACV has no payback |

**Primary commercial floor (Band A active GTM):** **≥ ₹30 lakh/month** electricity per plant.

**Nurture floor (Band B):** **₹15–30 lakh/month** — qualify aggressively; pilot only if champion strong.

**Band C:** **< ₹15 lakh/month** — research dossier OK for completeness; **no active outbound** unless strategic reference (award logo, IITR intro, adjacent plant intro).

## 2. Tier profiles (firmographic archetypes)

### 2.1 Band A — Primary GTM (“Large North India Manufacturer”)

| Dimension | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| **Monthly electricity bill** | **≥ ₹30L/plant** (sweet spot **₹50L–₹2Cr**) `[~]` |
| **Annual revenue** | **₹300–5,000 Cr** (single entity or plant-attributable) `[~]` |
| **Employees (plant)** | **500–5,000** `[~]` |
| **Geography** | **North India first** — NCR, Haryana, UP, Rajasthan, Punjab, HP, Uttarakhand |
| **Verticals** | Auto/components, cement, steel/metals, pharma, chemical, FMCG process, textiles (spinning) |
| **Infrastructure** | HT connection; EMS/SCADA likely; feeder or sub-metering common |
| **Buyer** | Plant Head / VP Ops + Head Electrical/Utilities/Energy |
| **Sales motion** | Qualify → bill review → **60-Day Proof Run** → annual contract **₹15–40L/yr** `[~]` |
| **Stamped positioning** | Read-only layer on EMS → rupee prescriptions → WhatsApp assignment → **verified with evidence** |

**Economic proof at ₹30L/month bill:**

| Savings % | Monthly ₹ | Annual ₹ |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| 10% | ₹3.0L | ₹36L |
| 15% | ₹4.5L | ₹54L |
| 20% | ₹6.0L | ₹72L |

**Positioning line:** *Large enough that every MD spike costs lakhs. Small enough that decisions happen at the plant, not a global procurement committee.*

### 2.2 Band B — Qualify-first (“Mid-scale process manufacturer”)

| Dimension | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| **Monthly bill** | **₹15–30L/plant** `[~]` OR unknown but scale signals strong |
| **Revenue** | **₹100–300 Cr** OR multi-plant SME with process intensity |
| **Geography** | Pan-India OK for research; **prioritize North India** plants within group |
| **Examples from SME100 2019** | Rhine Solar (Sonipat), Raaj Unocal (Faridabad), Pitambari satellite units, Kaynes EMS, Oceanic Foods (2 plants) |
| **Motion** | First call = **bill gate only**. If ≥₹30L → promote to Band A pipeline |
| **Risk** | Integration cost vs savings; owner-operator may want WhatsApp-only lightweight path |

**When to pursue Band B anyway:**
- Multi-plant land-and-expand (Plant 1 proves → Plant 2 is Band A)
- Process intensity extreme (foundry, smelting, spinning) at lower revenue
- Warm intro (IITR alumni, award network, DISCOM referral)
- ISO 50001 / PAT designated consumer with EnMS frustration

## 4. Buyer personas (8 deep profiles)

### 4.1 P-1: Head of Energy / Electrical / Utilities

**Titles:** VP Electrical, Head Energy Management, GM Utilities, Chief Electrical Engineer, DGM Energy, Head Electrical & Instrumentation

**Reports to:** Plant Head or COO

**Pain at scale:**
- MD penalties **₹5–15L+ per event** `[~]`
- EMS shows trends; **no closed-loop** to shift teams
- Feeder-level blame between production and utilities
- PAT/ISO audits need defensible SEC

**Stamped message:** *“Your EMS recorded the spike. We assign who fixes it and verify with evidence on the next bill cycle.”*

**Discovery questions:**
1. Last MD breach — cause, ₹ cost, who acted?
2. What does EMS **not** explain on the monthly bill?
3. SEC or kWh/ton — how often tracked?

**Pilot owner:** Yes — primary champion

---

### 4.2 P-2: Plant Head / BU Manufacturing Director

**Titles:** Plant Head, VP Operations, Unit Head, BU Director

**Pain:** Energy **8–20%** of conversion cost (auto) to **35–50%** (cement) `[~]`; board wants ₹ not charts

**Economic buyer** for **₹5–15L/quarter** pilot `[~]`

**Stamped message:** *“₹4.5L/month back at 15% on a ₹30L bill — verified with evidence, not projected.”*

**Rule:** Do not cold-CFO; route through Plant Head

---

### 4.3 P-3: MD / Owner / Promoter (SME100 common)

**Titles:** Managing Director, Director, Proprietor

**Pain:** Margin squeeze; tariff hikes; no internal energy analyst

**Stamped message:** *“90 days, one plant, kill criteria — no hardware capex. Show ₹ on bill or stop.”*

**Channel:** WhatsApp-first; shorter email; respect owner time

**SME100 note:** Many award winners are **promoter-led mid-caps** — owner may **be** the champion

---

### 4.4 P-4: CFO / Finance Controller

**Engaged when:** ACV > ₹15L/year or multi-plant rollout

**Needs:** IPMVP-style verification, opex not capex

**Lead with:** ₹ ledger from Proof Run, not AI

---

### 4.5 P-5: Maintenance / Head of Maintenance

**Titles:** Head Maintenance, Sr. Manager Maintenance, GM Engineering

**Pain:** Same equipment failures drive energy waste; CMMS tickets without ₹ link

**Stamped angle:** Pillar 2 prescriptions with energy co-benefit; not “predictive maintenance vendor”

---

### 4.6 P-6: Sustainability / ESG / Environment Head

**Use when:** BRSR parent, export OEM scorecards

**Lead with ₹**; ESG is supporting narrative

---

### 4.7 P-7: ISO 50001 / EnMS representative

**Titles:** Energy Management Rep, EnMS coordinator (often dual-hat engineer)

**Stamped angle:** Continuous automated EnMS vs quarterly spreadsheets; evidence-verified actions

**SME100:** Rare at SME scale unless auto supplier to OEMs

---

### 4.8 P-8: Technical Director / Founder-engineer

**Common in:** Chemical and specialty SME100 winners

**Approach:** Peer technical credibility (IITR); process-specific SEC hypothesis; respect their domain expertise

## 6. Bill estimation methodology (when bills unknown)

Use **triangulation** — never present as verified:

| Method | Formula / heuristic | Confidence |
|--------|---------------------|------------|
| **Annual report** | Power & fuel expense ÷ 12 | High if standalone mfg entity |
| **Capacity-based** | Spinning: ~₹800–1200/rotor/month `[~]`; Induction furnace: ₹15–40L/MW/month `[~]` | Medium |
| **Revenue intensity** | Chemical/pharma: 2–5% of revenue to electricity `[~]`; Auto forge: 3–8% `[~]` | Low–medium |
| **Employee proxy** | Heavy mfg: ₹15–40k/employee/month electricity `[~]` at 200+ employees | Low |
| **IndiaMART/MCA band** | Cross-check with plant sq ft and equipment lists | Low |

**SME100 2019 caveat:** Award winners skew **₹100–500 Cr** — many are **Band B/C** by bill, not Band A by logo.

## 7. Stamped product fit map (two pillars)

**Category:** Evidence-verified operational decision layer — **not** EMS replacement, **not** MES/CMMS.

| Pillar | Name | Primary outcomes | Typical Rx examples |
|--------|------|------------------|---------------------|
| **1** | Load & Energy Efficiency Intelligence | ₹ bill, MD, ToD, SEC | Stagger startups, setback schedules, tariff dispatch, idle load shutdown |
| **2** | Prescriptive Equipment Intelligence | Maintenance actions with energy co-benefit | Filter change, seal repair, lubrication, calibration |

**Commercial entry:** **60-Day Proof Run** — ₹2–5L fixed `[~]`; kill criteria at day 60; bill confirmation optional; Stamped telemetry evidence primary.

**Integration paths:**
- **Path A:** EMS/SCADA exists → read-only API/export + bill
- **Path B:** Meters + bills only → start narrow feeder scope

**Objection map:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “We have EMS” | Layer on top — prescriptions + verification with evidence |
| “We have ISO 50001” | Make EnMS continuous; auto-assign actions |
| “We have solar” | Solar fixes supply side; Stamped fixes **dispatch and waste** |
| “Send sustainability deck” | Plant P&L first |

## 9. Discovery & qualification scripts

### 9.1 Two-minute gate

| Question | Pass |
|----------|------|
| “Monthly electricity bill for **this** site?” | ≥ ₹30L (A) or ≥ ₹15L (B qualify) |
| “Energy managed here or at group HQ?” | Plant/BU |
| “EMS or SCADA today?” | Path A or B |
| “Who owns MD outcomes?” | Named person |

### 9.2 Intent scoring

| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| +3 | Bill ≥ ₹50L/month stated |
| +2 | MD penalty last quarter |
| +2 | EMS “monitoring only” frustration |
| +2 | PAT/BRSR deadline < 12 months |
| +1 | Captive solar/WHR underutilized |
| −2 | “Group IT evaluates next year” |
| −2 | Won’t share bill band |
