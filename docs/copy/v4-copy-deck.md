## Quelta `/products/process-quality`

- Eyebrow: Quelta · Process and Quality
- H1: More good parts from every line.
- Sub: Quelta joins every machine record to the result of each part, flags drift while a lot can still be saved, and proves each gain in your own numbers.
- Capabilities: One record for every part · Early warning while a lot can be saved · Likely causes, with the records behind them · Test on a model before the line · Proof against matched batches, energy per good part included · Maintenance flags with a suggested fix
- Maintenance spotlight: When a machine drifts from how it runs when healthy (more gas per kilo, a slower cycle, repeating micro-stops), Quelta raises a flag with the likely cause and the action it recommends, ranked by what a stop costs the line. Where early wear needs a sensor to see, it tells you where one would pay. Your maintenance head decides.
- How to start: Site survey · Replay of your worst lots · Paid pilot of 8 to 12 weeks · Annual subscription priced from the loss measured in the pilot
